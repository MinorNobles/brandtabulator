Brandtabulator

AI assistants and search engines describe brands using the public record: Wikipedia articles and the facts in Wikidata. This site shows, category by category, how complete and how current each brand's record is, and how often machines read it. It is updated every week.

It does not rank brands as products. It shows whose record is kept up, whose is not, and where the facts and the descriptions disagree.

Why this matters to a marketing team

When a customer asks an AI assistant which brand to buy, the answer leans heavily on these two public pages. A brand whose pages are thin, out of date or contradictory can be described wrongly, or left out, while a competitor with better-kept pages is named instead.

Your team can't see this from inside the company. This site shows where your brand stands against everyone else in its category, week by week, and what is missing.
